About

A future nurse, learning the floor first.

I'm a Patient Care Technician in the Cardiac ICU at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. The unit moves fast and the patients are critically ill, so most of the job is the unglamorous, necessary work: answering call lights and alarms, running fingersticks and EKGs, moving people safely between units, and supporting the family sitting in the corner chair.

I want to be a nurse, and I'd rather learn the floor firsthand than only read about it. Shadowing surgeries and then caring for those patients post-op has taught me what recovery actually looks like.

Outside the hospital I run enrichment programming for kids in Orchard Gardens and study stress-response and longevity in the Apfeld lab at Northeastern. Pediatrics is where I'd like to land — the kids are the reason.
